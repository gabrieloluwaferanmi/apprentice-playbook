# Week 1 - Environment & Diagnosis

## Expected Outcome

- Set up the local environment and verify it works.
- Intentionally break one configuration file.
- Use the terminal and AI assistance to diagnose and fix the issue.

## What To Show

- The failure you created.
- The reasoning that led to the fix.
- What the AI got right and what you had to verify yourself.

## Completion Check

- [ ] I can explain the root cause.
- [ ] I can show the repair in the repo.
- [ ] I documented the lesson learned.
