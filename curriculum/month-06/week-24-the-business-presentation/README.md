# Week 24 - The Business Presentation

## Expected Outcome

- Present the finished project to engineering and stakeholders.
- Explain the business value.
- Explain the technical debt and the maintenance plan.

## What To Show

- The finished product.
- The business narrative.
- What you would do differently with another month.

## Completion Check

- [ ] The story is clear to non-engineers.
- [ ] The tradeoffs are honest.
- [ ] The maintenance plan is realistic.
